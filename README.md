## About Me

I'm a **Computer Science graduate** interested in **urban analytics, transportation planning, and spatial data science**. I use Python and geospatial technologies to investigate urban systems, analyze accessibility, and explore data-driven approaches to urban challenges, particularly in **Addis Ababa and other rapidly growing African cities**.

## Featured Projects

### 🚌 Transit Accessibility Analysis — Addis Ababa
Combined OpenStreetMap transit data and WorldPop population estimates to investigate public transport proximity and spatial service gaps.

- 75.4% of estimated residents within 500 m of a mapped transit stop (straight-line distance).
- Identified 60 high-density underserved grid cells.

`Python` `GeoPandas` `Rasterio` `OSM` `WorldPop`

[View Research Repository](https://github.com/eyobazeze/transit-accessibility-projects)

### 🏥 Network Accessibility Analysis
Used a pedestrian street network to investigate walking access to healthcare facilities and schools in Addis Ababa.

- Approximately 71,880 network nodes.
- 131 hospitals and 347 schools analyzed.

`Python` `OSMnx` `NetworkX` `Geospatial Analysis`

[View Project](https://github.com/eyobazeze/network-accessibility-project)

### 🎨 UI/UX & Smart City Design
Designed civic-technology concepts addressing public transportation, housing, walkability, zoning, and urban feedback in Addis Ababa.

`Figma` `UI/UX` `Prototyping` `Civic Technology`
